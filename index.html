Every cue, to the second

Tap a cue and it plays right here from the right second. The bar at the bottom pauses, fades and shows the time. The ↗ button opens the same spot in the YouTube app as a backup.

Before the party

Set up the device

    Volume levels

    The page sets each cue's level by itself. To change a level, move the slider while a cue of that level plays. It remembers the new setting.

    iPhone: the page can't change the volume. Use the levels above as a guide. To fade, press volume-down about twice a second for 5 seconds, then tap Pause. Raise the volume again before the next cue.

    Do one sound check at the venue before guests arrive. Laptop keys: Space = pause or resume, F = fade, N = next song.
